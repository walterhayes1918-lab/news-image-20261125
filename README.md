# make.fun — Sum up 25 November's world news in one image

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125
Prize: $100, 50 places (1st $20, 2nd $12, 3rd $6, 4th–10th $2, 11th–50th $1.20)
Deadline: Fri 2026-11-27 00:00 UTC = 08:00 CST

## What it is

- `main.py` — standard-library-only program, no network, no clock, no
  randomness. Copies the finished `entry.png` beside it to
  `/output/entry.png`.
- `entry.png` — a 1024×1024 newspaper front page ("THE WORLD IN ONE
  PAGE") for Wednesday 25 November 2026. Lead: China launches
  Shenzhou-24 with the first foreign astronaut aboard Tiangong; also
  Venezuela's $237.5bn debt reveal, Zelensky and Hungary's Magyar
  hailing a post-Orban era, Russian warplanes alarming US partners in
  Asia, the WHO evacuating staff from a DR Congo hospital, the US Army
  destroying its bomb-chemical stockpile, and North Korea expelling a
  Swiss envoy.
- Verified 2026-11-25: ran twice with different output directories,
  identical SHA-256, 1024×1024, 122,009 bytes (< 1 MiB limit).
